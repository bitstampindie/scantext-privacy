# ScanText – OCR & PDF Scanner: Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 12 October 2026
**Developer/publisher:** Joydeep Chatterjee

**App:** ScanText – OCR & PDF Scanner
**Android package:** `com.scantext.scan_text`
**Privacy contact:** bitstampindie@gmail.com

## 1. About ScanText

ScanText is an Android application for scanning documents,
recognizing text in images and PDFs, creating PDF documents,
editing extracted text, and saving or sharing results.

This policy explains how ScanText handles documents, locally
stored information, and data associated with advertising.

## 2. Documents, images, and extracted text

ScanText accesses documents and images selected by you, or
captured using the camera, to provide its scanning and text
recognition features.

ScanText processes images and PDF pages on your device for
its core text-recognition features. ScanText does not send
your document contents or extracted text to servers operated
by the developer as part of those features.

PDF files and exported text may be saved on your device.
When you choose to share text or documents, Android and
the receiving application handle that user-directed transfer
according to the receiving application's practices.

## 3. Local storage and retention

ScanText stores a count of successful scans in Android local
application preferences. This counter is used to determine
when an interstitial advertisement becomes eligible to appear.
The counter is reset after an advertisement is shown.
This preference is stored on your device; ScanText does not
send the counter to a developer-operated server.

Generated PDFs can be stored in the application's documents
directory. They are not automatically deleted immediately
after creation.

Scanned-PDF text extraction may create temporary rendered
page images. The implementation includes cleanup of these
temporary files during normal completion or handled errors.
Unexpected interruption or operating-system termination may
prevent immediate cleanup.

Documents exported to locations you select, or shared with
other applications, may remain there independently of
ScanText. You control those copies through your device
and receiving applications.

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it ordinarily removes
its application-managed local data, subject to Android
behavior and backups. Files saved elsewhere are not
necessarily removed.

## 4. Advertising and associated data

ScanText integrates Google Mobile Ads to support advertising.

The advertising SDK may automatically process information
such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information,
and device or account identifiers, including advertising
identifiers when available.

These activities may support ad delivery, measurement,
analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

Advertising-related data may be transmitted to and
processed by Google and other participating advertising
partners according to applicable settings and policies.

Google's privacy information:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

ScanText's advertising configuration may change as the app
develops. This policy will be updated when its data-handling
practices materially change.

## 5. Advertising consent and privacy options

ScanText integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)
for advertising consent and privacy options.

Consent prompts and available privacy choices depend on
applicable law, region, and configured Google messages.

Where a privacy-options form is required and available,
ScanText provides an entry point for managing those options.
This Google consent-management interface is separate from
ScanText's Privacy Policy.

You can also review relevant Android privacy and advertising
settings on your device.

## 6. Permissions

ScanText uses camera access to capture documents.

File selection and export use Android's document and media
selection interfaces. ScanText does not use audio recording
for its scanning features.

Internet-related permissions are used by integrated
services including advertising and consent services.

## 7. Security

Core document processing is performed locally by the app.
The Google Mobile Ads SDK reports using TLS encryption
for the data it transmits.

No application or device can be guaranteed completely
secure. Users should protect access to their devices,
documents, and files shared with other applications.

Requests concerning data processed directly by Google may

## 9. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when ScanText's features,
integrations, or legal requirements change. An updated
effective date will accompany any published revision.
